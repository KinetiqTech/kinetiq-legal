KINETIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

KINETIQ is operated by Darius Hyde, California ("we", "us").

What stays on your phone

Your workouts, scores, weight log, routines and settings are stored on your device.

What we collect if you create an account

  • Your sign-in identity (Apple, Google, or email and password): a user ID, your email address (including a private relay address if you use Hide My Email), and your name if you share it.
  • Your display name and a friend code.
  • Weekly totals: average score, workouts, reps and streak.
  • Short activity lines, e.g. "scored 92 on SQUAT".
  • An optional backup of your workout history (including per-rep scores), only if you choose Back Up.
  • Nudges (slap, tap, high five, challenge, invite) you send or receive from friends: who sent it, who it went to, the kind and the time. Your setting for whether friends may nudge you.
  • If you allow notifications while signed in: a push address for your phone, supplied by Apple and Firebase, so a nudge can reach you.
  • Whether you have an active KINETIQ Pro subscription (yes or no), so your account knows. The App Store, not this record, decides what is unlocked.
  • Feedback you choose to send from the app: your message, rating, category, app version and your user ID.

Who can see it

  • Other signed-in KINETIQ users can see your display name, weekly totals and activity lines.
  • A person you nudge sees your display name and the kind of nudge. You can only nudge people whose friend code you added, only once an hour per person, and anyone can turn nudges off in Community.
  • Anyone who has a community invite link can see the community's name, tagline and the coach's display name on the invite page. Nothing about members is shown there.
  • If you join a coach's community, you agree to share with that coach: your display name, and each workout you finish after joining (exercise, sets, reps, rep scores and form problems), plus your weekly reps, average score, last workout time and streak. Your camera, journal and weight log are never shared. Leaving the community, being removed, or deleting your account deletes what was sent. If the coach turns on the leaderboard, other members of that community see your name, weekly reps, average score and streak.
  • If you start a community as a coach: its name, tagline, look, invite code, and the list of members and their workouts described above.
  • Your username, if you pick one. It is public to other signed-in people, who can search for it, and it is shown to friends, coaches and community members as your name.
  • Friend requests you send or receive: who sent it, who it went to and the time. A request is deleted once it is accepted or declined.
  • Workouts a coach assigns to a community you joined (title, note, exercises, due date) and whether you finished them.
  • Messages between you and a coach (text, time, sender, and the workout it refers to), the coach's announcements, and people you blocked. Messages are visible only to you and that coach.
  • Reports you send: the reported text, the reason and who it was about. Reports are reviewed by the KINETIQ team.
  • Only you can see your profile, friends list and backup.

Camera

Camera Coach uses the camera to find your body and count reps. Pictures are analyzed on your phone and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Microphone and speech

Voice commands such as "ready" are recognized on your phone while a workout is on screen. Audio is not recorded or sent.

Coach voice

To speak with a natural voice, the app sends the short coaching sentence (text only, no audio or personal details) to our voice server, which uses ElevenLabs to make the audio. If it is unavailable, your phone's own voice is used.

Coach memory, replay and journal

Your form habits (coach memory), the last worst-rep replay (joint points only, no video) and your journal (sleep, supplements, injuries, notes) are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Reminders and the hourly squat challenge are scheduled on your phone and nothing about them is sent to us. If you are signed in and allow notifications, your phone's push address is saved on your private profile so friend nudges can be delivered through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Subscriptions

KINETIQ Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we do not see your card details. If you are signed in, we keep a yes/no note of whether your subscription is active.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, KINETIQ writes finished workouts to Apple Health. You control this in Settings and in the Health app.

Bluetooth

Used only to connect to your KINETIQ module.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in Community: this removes your cloud data and sign-in. Workouts on your phone stay.
  • Sign out at any time.

Services we use

Google Firebase (authentication, database, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging), Apple Push Notification service, Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, friend code, weekly totals, activity lines, friends list, friend requests, username, nudges, push address, communities you run and your place in ones you joined (with the workouts and messages you sent), backup and Pro note, and your sign-in. Feedback you sent may be kept so we can improve the app.
