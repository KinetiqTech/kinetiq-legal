KINETIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

KINETIQ is operated by Darius Hyde, California ("we", "us").

What stays on your phone

Your workouts, scores, weight log, routines, goal, race plan, readiness score and settings are stored on your device. You can save a copy of all of it any time from Settings > Export my data.

What we collect if you create an account

  • Your sign-in identity (Apple, Google, or email and password): a user ID, your email address (including a private relay address if you use Hide My Email), and your name if you share it.
  • Your display name and a friend code.
  • Weekly totals: average score, workouts, reps and streak.
  • Short activity lines, e.g. "scored 92 on SQUAT".
  • An optional backup of your workout history (including per-rep scores), only if you choose Back Up.
  • Nudges (slap, tap, high five, challenge, invite) you send or receive from friends: who sent it, who it went to, the kind and the time. Your setting for whether friends may nudge you.
  • If you allow notifications while signed in: a push address for your phone, supplied by Apple and Firebase, so nudges, messages and coach notices can reach you, and your phone's time zone, so program reminders arrive in your morning.
  • Whether you have an active KINETIQ Pro subscription (yes or no), so your account knows. The App Store, not this record, decides what is unlocked.
  • Feedback you choose to send from the app: your message, rating, category, app version and your user ID.
  • When you create an account, we send you one welcome email at the address you signed up with.
  • If you use KAI's AI answers (part of KINETIQ Pro): a daily count of AI messages, so the daily limit works. The count is a number only.

Who can see it

  • Other signed-in KINETIQ users can see your display name, weekly totals and activity lines.
  • A person you nudge sees your display name and the kind of nudge. You can only nudge people whose friend code you added, only once an hour per person, and anyone can turn nudges off in Community.
  • Anyone who has a community invite link can see the community's name, tagline and the coach's display name on the invite page. Nothing about members is shown there.
  • If you join a coach's community, you agree to share with that coach: your display name, and each workout you finish after joining (exercise, sets, reps, rep scores and form problems), plus your weekly reps, average score, last workout time and streak. Your camera, journal and weight log are never shared. Leaving the community, being removed, or deleting your account deletes what was sent. If the coach turns on the leaderboard, other members of that community see your name, weekly reps, average score and streak.
  • Only if you turn on Settings > Share Readiness With My Coach: your readiness score and any pain or injury you logged in the last week (the area and how bad, 1 to 5). Only you and your coach can see it, never other members. Turning the setting off deletes what was shared.
  • If you start a community as a coach: its name, tagline, look, invite code, and the list of members and their workouts described above.
  • Your username, if you pick one. It is public to other signed-in people, who can search for it, and it is shown to friends, coaches and community members as your name.
  • Friend requests you send or receive: who sent it, who it went to and the time. A request is deleted once it is accepted or declined.
  • Workouts a coach assigns to a community you joined (title, note, exercises, due date, and the weeks of a program) and whether you finished them, plus any exercise swaps your coach sets for you.
  • Messages between you and a coach (text, time, sender, and the workout it refers to), the coach's announcements, and people you blocked. Messages are visible only to you and that coach.
  • Reports you send: the reported text, the reason and who it was about. Reports are reviewed by the KINETIQ team.
  • If you buy a coach plan: the App Store's signed purchase record (product, dates and transaction ID), which our server checks with Apple, plus your plan's tier, client limit and client count.
  • Only you can see your profile, friends list and backup.

Camera

Camera Coach uses the camera to find your body and count reps. Pictures are analyzed on your phone and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Microphone and speech

Voice commands such as "ready" and "pause" are recognized while a workout is on screen. If you use KAI's AI and leave "Hey KAI" on, the microphone also listens for "Hey KAI" while KINETIQ is open on your screen, never in the background. Speech is recognized on your phone when your iPhone supports it, and otherwise by Apple's speech recognition service. Audio is never recorded or sent to us. Only the words you say after "Hey KAI" are sent, as a message to KAI (see KAI AI below). With AirPods or another headset connected, its microphone may be used. You can turn "Hey KAI" off in Settings > KAI AI.

KAI AI (KINETIQ Pro)

KAI's AI answers are off until you agree to them in the app. When on, the messages you send KAI (typed or said after "Hey KAI") and a short summary of your training (recent workouts and form scores, your level, goal, readiness score, race plan and weight unit) are sent to our server and then to Anthropic, which makes the Claude model that writes KAI's reply. A short summary of your journal is included only if you turn that on separately. After a camera set, the coach may send that set's results the same way to give you a tip. Anthropic processes this under its commercial terms to produce the answer. We do not keep your messages; we keep only the daily count described above. You can turn AI answers off any time in Settings > KAI AI.

Reminders KAI sets

If you ask KAI to remind you, the reminder is scheduled as a notification on your phone. Nothing about it is stored by us.

Coach voice

To speak with a natural voice, the app sends the short coaching sentence (text only, no audio or personal details) to our voice server, which uses ElevenLabs to make the audio. If it is unavailable, your phone's own voice is used.

Coach memory, replay and journal

Your form habits (coach memory), the last worst-rep replay (joint points only, no video) and your journal (sleep, supplements, injuries, notes) are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Reminders and the hourly squat challenge are scheduled on your phone and nothing about them is sent to us. If you are signed in and allow notifications, your phone's push address is saved on your private profile so friend nudges can be delivered through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Coach plan renewals and cancellations are also sent to us directly by Apple, so a coach's client limit stays correct. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Subscriptions

KINETIQ Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we do not see your card details. If you are signed in, we keep a yes/no note of whether your subscription is active.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, KINETIQ writes finished workouts to Apple Health. If you connect Health for your readiness score, KINETIQ also reads your sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate and active calories. These are used on your phone to work out your readiness score and your heart rate during workouts. Health data is never sent to us, never used for advertising and never sold. The only exceptions are the readiness score itself, if you choose to share it with your coach, and the score included in the training summary KAI's AI sees. You control access in the Health app.

Crash reports and app usage

To find and fix bugs, KINETIQ uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. They receive crash reports (device model, iOS version, what the app was doing and a short log leading up to the crash), anonymous usage events such as "workout finished" with a number like a score or rep count, an app instance ID, and your account ID if you are signed in. They never receive your camera images, journal, Health data or messages. Turn this off any time in Settings > Share Crash Reports.

Bluetooth

Used only to connect to your KINETIQ module.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in Community: this removes your cloud data and sign-in. Workouts on your phone stay.
  • Sign out at any time.

Services we use

Google Firebase (authentication, database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase), Anthropic (KAI's AI answers), ElevenLabs (the coach's voice), Gmail (the welcome email), Apple Push Notification service, Apple speech recognition, Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, friend code, weekly totals, activity lines, friends list, friend requests, username, nudges, push address, communities you run and your place in ones you joined (with the workouts and messages you sent), backup and Pro note, readiness you shared with coaches, your AI message counts, and your sign-in. Feedback you sent may be kept so we can improve the app.
