KINETIQ Support

Need help with KINETIQ: Movement Coach? We are happy to help.

Contact

Email Kinetiqllc@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

  • Reps are not counting: place your phone so your whole body is in view, in good light, and keep the camera steady.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Restore a purchase: open KINETIQ Pro in the app and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Delete your data: workouts and settings are stored on your phone, so deleting the app removes them.
